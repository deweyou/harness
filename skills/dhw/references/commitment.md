# Commitment

A Commitment is the durable boundary between free exploration and governed
work. It records:

- objective and intended destination
- in-scope and explicit non-goals
- authority for mutation and external delivery
- acceptance Claim identities
- unresolved material decisions and their owner

Create a Commitment only when persistence adds value. Ask the user only when an
ambiguity changes behavior, data, cost, privacy, reversibility, or destination.

Every material change creates a new Commitment revision. Do not edit history.
Invalidate Claims that no longer describe the requested outcome, then propose a
new Plan against the new revision.
